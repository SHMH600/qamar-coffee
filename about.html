About Qamar Coffee Roasters

A Doha family business with a passion for exceptional coffee.

Who we are

From a market stand to a roastery

In 2021 Noora started roasting coffee in a tiny kitchen in Doha and selling bags to friends through Instagram. Word spread, and soon a weekend market stand could not keep up with the orders.

In 2024 we opened our own roastery in Al Sadd. Today we roast every week in small batches so that each bag reaches you at its peak of flavour.

  • 2021: first bags sold on Instagram
  • 2023: market stand at Souq Waqif weekends
  • 2024: our own roastery opens in Al Sadd
  • 2026: our first online shop

Our mission

To make specialty coffee warm, simple and welcoming for everyone in Qatar, by roasting fresh, sourcing fairly and teaching people to brew well at home.

Our vision

To become the most loved local coffee brand in the Gulf, known for quality, honesty and a premium experience in every cup.
